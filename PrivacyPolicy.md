# Privacy Policy

**I Wanna Aikido Exam** ("the App") is a free, open-source iOS application.

## Data We Collect
The App does **not** collect, transmit, or share any personal data.
All settings and profiles are stored locally on your device using Apple's UserDefaults and SwiftData frameworks.

## Analytics
The App contains no analytics SDKs and makes no network requests.

## Third-Party Services
None.

## Children
The App does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children.

## Changes
Any future changes to this policy will be reflected in an updated date above and committed to this repository.
